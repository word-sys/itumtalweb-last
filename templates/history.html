Our History

Our school, which dates back to the 17th century and is known as Ortaköy, has hosted the dreams of many well-educated generations until today. Our building, which gained a new importance with the construction of Çırağan Palace, was unfortunately out of service for a while due to a fire that broke out in the palace.

It rose from its ashes in 1928 and became the "High Maritime Trade School" and began to play a major role in our country's maritime tradition. In 1982, our buildings and facilities were transferred to the Ministry of National Education to train middle-level seafarers. At that time, it was called Ortaköy Maritime Vocational High School; The "Deck" and "Ship Machinery" departments also became operational and started to accept free boarding students.

In the 1983-84 academic year, the Department of "Marine Electronics and Communications" was added to our school. In 1993, M.E.B. Our school was named after the late Ziya Kalkavan, who worked as a manager and manager in the maritime industry for more than half a century, and with its new name; It became "Beşiktaş Ziya Kalkavan Anatolian Maritime Vocational High School".

In 2015, it was changed to Ziya Kalkavan Vocational and Technical Anatolian High School. ITU Vocational and Technical Anatolian High School was established in 2019 with a protocol made between MEB and ITU in the same area. Istanbul Technical University reinterpreted its academics and experience within the framework of high school education in order to train qualified personnel for the sector.

Today, ITU MTAL meets with students who are ambitious about the future with its opportunities and innovative excitement.
